Top three activities to do in Lochem

Explore the Town Square

Stroll through Lochem's charming town square, admire historic buildings, relax in cozy cafés, enjoy local cuisine at restaurants, and mingle with friendly locals.

Forest Walks

Escape into nature with scenic walks through the surrounding forests, breathe in fresh air, and soak up the peaceful environment.

Sports and Activities

Try out a variety of sports in Lochem, from soccer, hockey, and badminton to taekwondo. There us something for every fitness level and interest.
